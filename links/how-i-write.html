How I Write

This site has two sections with different relationships to AI.

Main blog

The reflective essays on the main blog are written by me. I draft on paper, dictate the text, then use AI for grammar, typos, and a light review pass. AI does not rewrite my ideas. I reject suggestions that would change my voice.

I wrote about that review workflow in the devlog: How I Use AI to Write Without Losing My Voice.

Devlog

The devlog is different. It is AI-assisted by design, like a rubber duck that remembers things. I capture what I am learning, trying, or breaking in the moment. Less polish, more velocity. The pilot post explains why I started it.

That is the split. Essays here are mine. Notes there are faster and more AI in the loop.
